The site you are reading. It will mostly hold long posts, so I didn't want a plain blog template; the theme comes from my interest in sailing. The home page opens on an Aegean chart generated from code, posts are listed like logbook entries, and my career is drawn as a course plotted on the chart. The colours come from real nautical charts: chart paper, deep water and the magenta of lighthouses.

It is built with Hugo and served from Cloudflare Workers. The chart and the drawings on each page are SVGs generated with Python; they take their colours from CSS, so they follow the light and dark themes. There is no analytics or tracking, and comments live in GitHub Discussions. I built it with Claude in about an hour.
